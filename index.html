🌕 A Guide To Volleyball



Volleyball

Volleyball is a team sport, played outdoors or indoors. It consists of 6 on court players. The roles consists of setter, outside hitter, opposite hitter, middle blocker, and the libero.


Basic Rules

  • 6 players per team on the court.
  • 3 hits max per side before sending the ball over.
  • Stay outside the court when serving.
  • Don't touch the net or step fully over the center line.

  • Court shoes
  • Net
